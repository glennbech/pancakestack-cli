# pancakestack-cli

Solo-founder project. No ceremony, no PRs.

- Commit and push directly to `main`. Always.
- PRs are not used here — do not open one, do not suggest one, do not
  offer one as an option. Asking "should I open a PR?" is the wrong
  question; the answer is permanently no.
- Push release tags right after `main` is up to date — that's what
  triggers goreleaser.
- Don't ask before pushing to main; the answer is always yes.
